Bio

Rachel Yan is a junior at Parsons School of Design, majoring in Communication Design and minoring in Economics. She's a designer and creative coder with an insatiable need to experiment with new techniques and technologies.

Her work focuses on using interactivity in its various forms to create engaging and unique experiences, while also designing cohesive and engaging visual systems that can span across a variety of mediums. She believes in simplifying complex systems through meaningful and detail oriented design, in order to convey any message possible.
